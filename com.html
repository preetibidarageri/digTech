What is Digital Marketing ?

Digital marketing is the promotion of products or services using online channels, including websites, social media, search engines, and email, to reach a targeted audience in real time. It is more cost-effective and measurable than traditional methods, allowing for precise, data-driven targeting across devices like smartphones and laptops.
